THE FOUNDATIONAL PROBLEM

The Hallucination Dilemma in Modern Research

Why ungrounded language models pose severe risks to researchers, students, and engineers.

✕

Typical Conversational AI

  • Parametric Confabulation: Answers from static, compressed training weights with zero real-time document verification.
  • Fabricated Bibliography: Confidently invents non-existent arXiv IDs, phantom author collaborations, and fake publication years.
  • Compulsive Answering: Attempts to answer questions even when completely lacking domain evidence, masking ignorance with persuasive rhetoric.
  • Untraceable Assertions: You cannot pinpoint which paragraph or page in the original paper justified a specific mathematical claim.
✓

AI Research Navigator

  • Non-Parametric Grounding: Every response is generated exclusively from retrieved passages within the verified research corpus.
  • Audited Section Citations: Every sentence is tagged with markers like [1] pointing directly to paper section titles and ArXiv links.
  • Deterministic Refusal Policy: If fewer than 2 relevant chunks exist, the system safely refuses rather than hallucinating unsupported claims.
  • Dual-Engine Hybrid Fusion: Combines Sentence-Transformers dense semantics with BM25Okapi lexical matching for acronym accuracy.
